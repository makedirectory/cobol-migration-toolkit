# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please report it
privately. **Do not open a public issue.**

- Email **andrew@mk-dir.com** with details and, if possible, a proof of concept.
- Alternatively, use GitHub's private **"Report a vulnerability"** flow under the
  repository's **Security** tab.

Please include:

- A description of the issue and its impact
- Steps to reproduce or a proof of concept
- Affected version(s) or commit

## What to Expect

- We aim to acknowledge your report within **3 business days**.
- We follow a **90-day coordinated disclosure** window. We will work with you on
  a fix and public disclosure timeline, and will credit you unless you prefer to
  remain anonymous.

## Scope

These projects are educational starters. Please pay particular attention to:

- Command/argument injection across the process boundary
- Input validation and resource-exhaustion (timeouts, size limits)
- Handling of untrusted COBOL source files

Thank you for helping keep the community safe.
